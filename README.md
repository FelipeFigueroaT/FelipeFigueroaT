## 👨‍🔬 About Me

Hi! I'm a PhD student at the [Instituto de Física y Astronomía, Universidad de Valparaíso](https://ifa.uv.cl/), Chile, and a member of the **Massive Stars Group**. My work focuses on the physics of the most luminous stars in the universe — how they lose mass through powerful radiation-driven winds.

## 🔭 Research Interests

- **O-type stars** and their radiation-driven stellar winds
- **Self-consistent hydrodynamic models** using the **m-CAK theory**
- Mass-loss rates (Ṁ) beyond the classical β-law parametrization
- Effect of **photospheric chemical composition** on the line-force
- **Slow solutions** and alternative hydrodynamic wind formalisms
- State-of-the-art atmosphere codes: **FASTWIND** and **PoWR**

> My goal is to bridge complex hydrodynamics and practical formulae that the wider community can use to understand stellar evolution.
